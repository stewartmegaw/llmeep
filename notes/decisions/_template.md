# DEC-000 — <title>

## Status

`accepted` — as of <YYYY-MM-DD>.

Values: `accepted`, `superseded`. A decision under discussion is not yet a decision; keep it
in `.notes/` until it is made.

## Context

The situation that forced a choice. What was true, what was constraining, what would have
happened if nothing was decided. Enough that a reader who was not there can judge whether
the reasoning still holds.

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly and in the present tense. One paragraph.

## Alternatives considered

The rejected options and the specific reason each was rejected. **This section is the
value of the note** — without it, a future reader cannot tell whether the alternative was
weighed and dismissed or simply never seen.

- **<Alternative>** — rejected because <reason>.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it commits us to. Include the costs
accepted knowingly.

## Revisit when

The conditions under which this should be reopened — a scale threshold, a dependency
changing, a date. If nothing would change your mind, say so.
